ABOUT THE PROJECT

Designed to explore proactive maintenance.

MotoSense AI is a student portfolio prototype demonstrating full-stack development, data handling, and explainable risk-flag logic.

⌘

Data capture

Records odometer, days since service, engine temperature, vibration readings, and notes.

◈

Explainable flags

Shows which demo thresholds contributed to a Low, Medium, or High flag.

▤

Service analytics

Summarises records and exports data to CSV for further analysis.

Data and safety: No Royal Enfield internal data or official specifications are included. The prototype's rules are illustrative and are not validated mechanical diagnostics. A real version would require permissioned, representative sensor/service data, expert-defined labels, rigorous validation, and safety review.
